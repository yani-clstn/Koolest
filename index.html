Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how Koolest AC Services collects, uses, and protects your personal information when scheduling service appointments or submitting feedback.

1. Information We Collect

When booking a service or submitting feedback, we collect limited personal details:

  • Full Name (for client identification)
  • Contact Details (Email and Phone number for appointment confirmation and status updates)
  • Service Location (Address/City for technician dispatching)
  • Feedback & Ratings (Optional comments submitted by clients)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected details strictly for essential operational purposes:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and updating your service appointments.
  • Direct contact regarding technician arrival times or service details.
  • Improving overall customer service and platform reliability.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only accessed by authorized personnel to carry out requested service calls and is stored securely using cloud infrastructure.

4. Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or removal of your personal records at any time by contacting our support team.
